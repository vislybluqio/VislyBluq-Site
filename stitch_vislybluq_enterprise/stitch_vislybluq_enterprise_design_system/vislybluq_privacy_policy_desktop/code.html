Introduction

VislyBluq Enterprise Technology Consulting ("we", "our", "us") operates the website and various enterprise consulting platforms. We value the privacy of our clients and partners above all else.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information that you provide to us when you visit our website, use our software, or engage our consulting services. By accessing our platform, you agree to the terms outlined herein.

02

Data Collection

person Personal Information

We collect identifiable information such as names, email addresses, job titles, and company affiliations when you voluntarily submit them through contact forms or service registrations.

analytics Usage Telemetry

Our systems automatically collect technical data including IP addresses, browser types, interaction heatmaps, and session durations to improve our platform performance.

We do not collect sensitive biological, political, or religious data unless explicitly required for specific government-vetted projects and with separate, verified consent.
